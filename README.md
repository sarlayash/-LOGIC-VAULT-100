## 💎 Key Features

### 1. 👑 Signature Black & Gold Design System
- Cyber-gold obsidian aesthetic with metallic gold borders, glassmorphic cards, and zero formatting/cropping issues on mobile, tablet, and laptop screens.
- Live ticking real-time clock in header with local timezone and date.
- Sound FX synthesizer using Web Audio API (satisfying golden chimes, level-up fanfares, and mute toggle).
- Confetti particle canvas upon milestones and completions.

### 2. 📚 100 Complete Problem Statements Across 12 Domains
1. **Banking, FinTech & Insurance** (001 - 009)
2. **Healthcare, Hospitals & Pharma** (010 - 018)
3. **Retail, E-commerce & Consumer Goods** (019 - 027)
4. **Manufacturing, Supply Chain & Logistics** (028 - 036)
5. **Education, EdTech & Workforce Development** (037 - 044)
6. **IT, SaaS, Cybersecurity & Cloud Operations** (045 - 052)
7. **Government, Public Services & Smart Cities** (053 - 060)
8. **Energy, Utilities & Sustainability** (061 - 068)
9. **Travel, Hospitality, Aviation & Mobility** (069 - 076)
10. **Agriculture, Food Systems & Rural Enterprise** (077 - 084)
11. **Telecom, Media & Digital Platforms** (085 - 092)
12. **HR, Professional Services & Enterprise Operations** (093 - 100)

### 3. 🎯 Full 8-Point Submission Standard for Every Problem
- **1. Problem Understanding & Business Objective**
- **2. Assumptions & Business Rules**
- **3. Input / Output Design Table**
- **4. Logic & Algorithmic Ordered Steps**
- **5. Production Pseudocode** (with 1-Click Copy)
- **6. 5 Realistic Edge Cases**
- **7. 5 Test Cases**
- **8. Complexity & Limitations**
- ⚡ **Interactive Logic Simulator:** Test parameters live in-browser.
- 📝 **Learner Scratchpad:** Personal notes saved in LocalStorage.

### 4. 📝 5 Mock Tests + 1 Grand Final Assessment
- **Mock Test 1:** Banking, FinTech & Retail Architecture (10 questions)
- **Mock Test 2:** Healthcare, Hospitals & Public Systems (10 questions)
- **Mock Test 3:** Supply Chain, Logistics & Energy Operations (10 questions)
- **Mock Test 4:** IT, Cloud, CyberSec & Telecom Scalability (10 questions)
- **Mock Test 5:** EdTech, HR & Enterprise Workflow Logic (10 questions)
- **Grand Final Assessment:** 20 comprehensive questions spanning all 12 domains, timed exam interface, instant scoring, question explanations, and 75% pass mark requirement.

### 5. 🏆 Gamification & Official Credential
- **10 Engineering Progression Tiers:** From *Logic Novice* up to *Grandmaster of Systems*.
- **18 Badges:** 6 Special Achievement Seals + 12 Domain Mastery Badges.
- **Official Verifiable Gold Certificate:** Displays Learner Name, Issue Date, Unique Verification Credential ID (`KLV-2026-XXXXX`), Kapil's signature, and 1-Click Print/Save as PDF formatted for A4 landscape.

### 6. 📲 100% Offline Progressive Web App (PWA)
- Installable as a native app on Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.
- Powered by `manifest.webmanifest` and `sw.js` Service Worker.
- Zero external server dependencies; all logic runs offline in client.

### 7. ⚖️ 100 Marks Evaluation Rubric Included
- Problem Interpretation (15 Marks)
- Business Rules & Assumptions (15 Marks)
- Logic Quality (25 Marks)
- Pseudocode Clarity (20 Marks)
- Edge Cases & Test Cases (15 Marks)
- Complexity & Risk Awareness (10 Marks)
